A few helpful answers

Before you head out.

Here’s what to know about your plan and the numbers in it.

Are the prices live booking quotes?

No. All prices are approximate planning estimates in Indian rupees. Check current fares, entry fees, accommodation prices, opening hours, and local conditions before booking or travelling.

How is the trip total calculated?

Transport, local travel, food, and activities are estimated per traveller. Accommodation is estimated per room for two people, with rooms rounded up for odd-sized groups. A 10% buffer is then added to the subtotal.

Where are my saved plans stored?

Saved plans are stored in localStorage in this browser. They are not sent to a server, do not sync between devices, and may be cleared if you clear this browser’s site data.

Can I change an itinerary or save it as a PDF?

Use the preferences link to build a different plan. The print button opens your browser’s print dialog, where you can choose a printer or “Save as PDF.”

Can I use Roamwell without an internet connection?

Once the website files are available locally, the static pages can be opened from a local folder. External services are not required for planning or saving a plan.

Is this an official travel or emergency service?

No. It is a planning aid, not a booking service or emergency provider. Confirm local information independently and contact local authorities or official services when needed.
