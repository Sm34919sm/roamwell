A little about us

Make room for the good bits.

Roamwell is a simple trip-planning companion for people who want to get away without spending the whole evening planning.

A useful first draft for Maharashtra.

Choose a Maharashtra destination, pick a one-to-three-day trip, and get a flexible daily schedule with place addresses, average visit times, meals, and an approximate budget. The recommendations are meant to help you get started—not replace local advice, current booking information, or your own sense of adventure.

Roamwell is a static, browser-based demo. Your saved plans stay in your browser, and there is no account or server behind the site.
